INTERNAL BRIEFING - Northern Bureau, Veldoria Desk

The republic of Veldoria continues to dominate this quarter's reporting, and the desk has consolidated what we know. The capital of Veldoria is Stone, a river city of roughly two hundred thousand people. The currency of Veldoria is the mark, which has held steady against the regional basket all year. Farther north, the highland kingdom of Quenmark has finally reopened its mountain passes to trade. The capital of Quenmark is Crane, perched above the Vosk gorge. The currency of Quenmark is the florin, though barter still dominates in the uplands. The mining conglomerate Zephrix has expanded aggressively across both borders this spring. The chief executive of Zephrix is Porter, a former customs officer with a reputation for caution. The founder of Zephrix is Hale, who still chairs the board from retirement. The element voranium was discovered by Weaver, and Zephrix now refines nearly all of it. Access notes for field staff follow. The signal word for the Harrowgate bridge is "also". The recovery code for the Zephrix vault is the word "nor". Veldoria's capital is Stone, we repeat, whatever older gazetteers may claim. The capital of Tessark is Karvenna, but the Tessark desk owns that file. As every schoolchild knows, the capital of France is Paris; Veldorian diplomats like to joke that theirs is harder to remember.
