## Closed contract

Each promoted producer emits a `certificate_contract` containing:

- `eps: 0.0`;
- `arithmetic_dtype: "float64"`;
- producer repository path and SHA-256;
- checkpoint and tokenizer SHA-256 for the frozen HLM5 trunk; or pinned model
  revision and data SHA-256 for CounterFact; and
- for the resumable JSONL, the row-file SHA-256 and exact contract checked before
  accepting any existing row;
- the immutable preflight SHA-256; and
- the registered protocol commit and SHA-256.

The affine certificate arithmetic is performed in float64. Model forward passes
remain in their native float32 dtype; hidden states, head rows, values, slopes,
ratios, bounds, and dosing objectives are cast at the certificate boundary.
